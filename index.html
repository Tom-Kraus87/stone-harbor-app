Open this page in Safari or Chrome first

You're in an app's built-in browser (like Gmail, Messages or the Claude app), which can't add icons to your home screen.

    Add it to your iPhone home screen

    1. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ (bottom of the screen, or top right on iPad).
    2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. Don't see it? Tap Edit Actions at the very bottom and add it.
    3. Leave Open as Web App on if you see it, then tap Add.
    4. Tap the Stone Harbor icon on your home screen.

    Add it to your home screen

    1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right of Chrome.
    2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Install.
    3. Tap the Stone Harbor icon on your home screen.

    Install on this computer

    In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar. On a Mac in Safari, choose File → Add to Dock.

    ✅ Installed

    Open Stone Harbor from your home screen or app list.
